### 5. GLOBAL-04 (with ORCH-01, TEST-05), SHOULD: `driver/vz/driver.go:237`

This is unchanged since r1. qemu (`driver.go:221`), firecracker (`driver_linux.go:218`) and vz (`driver.go:237`) each pass `req.Link().Claim()` without a tier-0 test that the claim carries the link. Only Hyper-V has one. Dropping the link is loud on qemu and firecracker, where the relay answers 400 'build_link is required'. On vz it is quiet: host mode falls back to its `--subnet` default.

**Suggested fix:** add one assertion per driver that the claim carries the request's link. The order-test seams can capture the ClaimRelay arguments. At minimum cover vz.
