## Tier 2 findings

### MUST

**1. RELAY-05 (hybrid), `specs/metadata-http-body/v1.md:31`: the deviation note names the wrong releases.**

The note says that only v0.6.0 and unreleased builds from `b716d9e` carry `path`. In fact, v0.2.0 to v0.5.0 also write it on every build-context-file close, under the same `schemas/v1/http-body.json` `$id` with `additionalProperties: false`. A third-party reader of this long-lived spec would wrongly treat those ledgers as strictly conformant.

The same false premise appears in three other places:
- the design's History paragraph (`docs/design/http-body-path-drift.md:39-42`: "v0.5.0 had no such field");
- D3 (`:128`) and plan step 4 (`:300-301`);
- the a1/a2 question text that Jeff confirmed.

The decision does not change. Option C's own argument, that old ledgers stay non-conforming to the v1 schema they name, only gets stronger, and "readers ignore it" and "metadata is unsigned" still hold.

**Fix (wf-implement), in a new commit with no amend or force-push:**
- Rewrite the note to say that ledgers from v0.2.0 to v0.6.0 carry `path` on each build-context-file close, and that v0.6.0 also carries it on the build-script and bootstrap closes. Keep the rest of the note.
- Commit that file alone, with a fresh trailer such as `Tier1-Allow: RELAY-05 specs/metadata-http-body/v1.md: correct the releases the deviation note names; schema unchanged (http-body-path-drift)`.
- Correct the design at `:39-42`, `:128` and `:300-301`.
- In the PR body, say that a1 and a2 were confirmed on the wrong premise and that this does not change the choice.
