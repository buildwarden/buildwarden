## Findings

### 1. UX-01 (MUST): the Hyper-V guide still sets the image ages in `[vm]`

**Where:** `docs/book/src/guide/drivers.md:462`. Related: the design at `docs/design/driver-contract.md:3434`.

**What is wrong:** the Hyper-V section still says "Both ages are set in `[vm]` (`image_refresh_after`, `image_min_grace`)". K1 renames those keys:

- The new keys are `[driver] image_refresh_after` and `image_min_grace`, and `[driver.hyperv]` overrides them for hyperv.
- `[vm]` is now the deprecated old name and warns when used (`driver/drivercmd/settings.go:166-171`, `configuration.md:227-234`).

The design requires that "`guide/drivers.md` names the new keys in each driver's section". The qemu section (`drivers.md:252`) was updated; the Hyper-V section was not.

The line was already wrong at `dd370cb` and the previous report missed it. The merge did not cause it: #131 only added the two sentences before it.

**Fix:** name the new keys, for example: "Both ages are set in `[driver]` (`image_refresh_after`, `image_min_grace`), or in `[driver.hyperv]` for hyperv only; `[vm]` is their old name."

### 2. TEST-04 (MUST): the closed-output teardown test orders its events with a 200 ms sleep

**Where:** `driver/drivercmd/drivercmd_test.go:590`, and the closes at `:626-628`.

**What is wrong:** TestRunTeardownWithClosedOutput (DRV-51) depends on timing:

1. teardownDriver waits for the end of its input.
2. It sleeps 200 ms "to let the orchestrator's ends of stdout and stderr close".
3. It writes 100 times to stdout and stderr.

The test closes stdin first and closes stdout and stderr after it. On a loaded or `-race` runner the writes can happen while the pipes are still open. They then succeed, since the pipes hold far more than these lines, and the test passes without testing closed output.

The test cannot go red this way, but whether it tests DRV-51 depends on wall-clock timing.

**Fix:** close stdout and stderr before stdin in the test, since the driver notices only the end of its input, and drop the sleep. The order is then fixed.

### 3. GLOBAL-06 (MUST-review): two security boundaries change

**Where:** `cmd/warden/driverlookup.go:17`, `cmd/warden/buildseq.go:381`, the design's Security note (line 6407). Related: TEST-08, FLOW-08.
