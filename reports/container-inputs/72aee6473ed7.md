- The first Decision still says `RecordInputs` is "called by every driver after `FinalizeOutput`".

The archived design (FLOW-12) would misdescribe the change.

**Suggested fix.** Change lines 46-47 to "called by every driver right after staging the build script". Alternatively, drop the timing from that bullet and leave it to the "Recorded at staging" Decision.
